# Paper type: Survey / expository

- Broader titles and longer introductions are acceptable here; what is forbidden in a research paper (field-wide scope, historical sweep) is the survey's job.
- Still one theme: a survey organizes a field around a clear thread, it does not enumerate everything.
- A table of contents or a detailed "organization" paragraph replaces the short research-paper outline; for long surveys, do both only if the journal expects it.
- Abstracts of surveys may stay brief and need not claim a new theorem or method; they must still state the scope and the intended reader.
- Attribution discipline is stricter: every named result needs a citation; opinions about the field's direction must be marked as such.
