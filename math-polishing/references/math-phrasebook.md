# Math phrasebook (deep reference — load on demand)

Condensed stock patterns for mathematical English. Use to suggest idiomatic replacements, not to stuff a draft with formulas.

## 1. Core sentence patterns

- Let $f$ be a continuous function. / Suppose (that) $g$ is differentiable. / We define a problem to be stable if ...
- If $\alpha > -1$, then the integral exists.
- Solving the above system, we have ... / Solving equations (2.1) and (2.2) gives (yields) ... / A simple manipulation leads to the solutions ...
- Solving for $x_1$ in terms of $x_2$, we have ... / Rearranging the system, we obtain ... / Eliminating $x_2$ from Eq. (2.2) gives ...
- Substituting it into the first equation gives rise to ... / Inserting (3) into (4) gives ... / If we plug (6) back into (1), we obtain ...
- Using Eq. (1.1), we obtain ... / Using the fact that $a > 0$, we have ...
- Without loss of generality we assume that $x > 0$. / It suffices to prove the theorem in the case that ... / We need only consider the case ...
- It follows that ... / It follows from the above equation that $u = v$.
- According to formula (4), the equation is solvable. / Thanks to the identity, (3) is proved.
- It is easy to see (show, prove, verify, check) that ... / It is apparent from the triangle inequality that ...
- A lower bound for $h$ can be obtained. / A routine computation gives rise to the formula.
- Rather than give a detailed proof, we outline it. / Our goal is to show the uniqueness of the solution.
- We will restrict ourselves to two dimensions. / We omit the proof due to its tediousness.
- ... is referred to as the entropy. / An alternative way to view it is to use formula (3).
- We would rather prove it here than refer to [3]. (取舍语气:宁可自证,不甩引用)
- No matter what $x$ is, $y$ is always nonzero. / $g'$ will decrease, even if $g$ increases.
- Substitute ... for $x$ in formula (3.2). — substitute A for B = 用 A 取代 B,方向易反。
- Passive variant when the method, not the operator, is the point: "The solutions can be easily obtained via an elementary method." — via 后接方法名,不接人。
- Downgrade rule: "Because of the fact that ..." is wordy — replace with because/since.

## 2. Math operation verbs (gerund + gives/we have/we obtain)

- Differentiating both sides of Eq. (1) with respect to $x$ gives the desired estimate (2).
- Integrating the first term of Eq. (1) by parts, we have ... / Changing the order of integration and integrating with respect to $x$, we obtain ...
- Combining Lemma 1.1 and Theorem 2.1, we see that ... / Equating the corresponding coefficients of (1) and (2) gives ...
- Separating the variables and integrating, we have ... / Expanding the right side of (4) into power series, we have ...
- Treating $U$ as a new variable, we have ... / Introducing the change of variable ..., we have ...
- Denoting the determinant of $A$ by $D$ and using (1), one has ...
- Summing over $m$, we have ... / Taking out a factor $a_{11}$ from all terms involving $X_1$, we have ...
- Applying Theorem 1 to (2), we obtain ... (or: An application of Theorem 1 to (2) gives ...)
- Taking the Fourier transform of both sides, we have ... / Interchanging the operations of taking the limit and integrating, we have ...
- Equating real and imaginary parts of this equation, we obtain ... / By comparing (1) with (2), we obtain $u = v$.
- Continuing by induction, we finally have ... / Multiplying both sides by $-1$, adding $A$ to both sides and dividing by $k$, we get ...

Useful verbs: assume, establish, develop, derive, carry out, collect/regroup, prove/show, assert/claim, generalize/extend, illustrate/demonstrate, investigate, verify, sketch/plot, split/decompose, expect/predict, summarize, observe/find, imply/suggest/indicate, remark/point out, solve/resolve, employ, confirm, incorporate, correct, revise.

More gerund patterns:

- Setting the coefficient of $x^3$ to zero, we have ... / Retaining the first two terms in the power series, we find ...
- Performing the same procedure for $j$ from 1 to $m$, we have ... / Averaging over $X_i$, we see ...
- Using Eq. (3) in Eq. (2), we have, in light of Eq. (1), ... (in light of = 借助/鉴于,作插入语)
- Noting that the average values of $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ are zero by definition, we have ...
- Extracting the coefficient of $S^n$ from both sides, we can show ... / Separating real and imaginary parts of Eq. (1) gives rise to ...
- Assuming that Eq. (1) holds (is valid), we have $u = v$. — holds / is valid 表"成立"两选。

Operation verb/noun pairs (watch the traps):

- computation/evaluation — calculate/compute/evaluate (evaluate 求值, compute 计算)
- differentiation/derivative — differentiate/take the derivative; integration/integral — integrate
- equality — equate (equate A with/to B); deformation — deform; elimination — eliminate; expansion — expand; decomposition — decompose; transformation — transform
- factorization — factorize; triangulation — triangulate; quantification — quantify
- performance — perform (performance 另有"性能"义: "The performance of the algorithm is consistent with the theoretical analysis.")
- note/notion — notice (note=注记, notion=概念,勿混); treatment — treat ... as; extraction — extract; reservation/preservation — reserve/preserve/retain (retain = 截断保留)
- picture/graph/figure — sketch/plot/graph/draw (sketch 草图, plot 精确作图)

More verbs with published-sentence anchors: arrive at (arrive at the conclusion), arrange (Arranging the terms in a natural order gives ...), intend (We intend to solve the conjecture in the paper), mean (This means that the equation is unsolvable), accept (The hypothesis is accepted by most researchers), qualify (be qualified to be called ...), produce (This produces an alternative proof).

- It is not difficult to verify that the estimates of Section 2 are satisfied by the solutions of equation (5). — estimates are satisfied by solutions,主语选择决定段落焦点。
- We prove the theorem by first investigating the smoothness of the solution. / The validity of estimate (5) can be demonstrated by Example 1.

## 3. Transitions (paragraph openers)

- We now turn to the mathematical formulation of the problem.
- With the help of the preceding two lemmas we can now prove the following extension of Theorem 2.
- However, another question arises: ...? To answer it, we need the following lemma.
- Before approaching Problem 1, we first ask ...
- Up to now we have studied ... . However, ...
- We conclude this section with the proof of the maximum principle in bounded domains. / We conclude this section by pointing out that ...
- Before proceeding further, let us remark on ...
- Next we ask the following question ... / In conclusion, we have proved the following theorem ...
- The crucial result in our development of local properties of weak solutions will be the following weak Harnack inequality. (预告关键结果)
- The next theorem, also due to Smith, deals with the case in which the ratio $\lambda$ is negative. (归属插入语 also due to X)
- We finally remark that ... (段末收束)

## 4. Proof language

### Openings
- The aim (idea) is to ... / Our first goal is to show that ... / The trick of the proof is to find ...
- It suffices to establish the estimate (1) for $x = 0$.
- We first show that ... / We proceed to prove this theorem. / We are now turning to the proof of Theorem 1.
- Proceeding as in the proof of Theorem 1, we have ... / By suitable modification of the proof of Theorem 1, we can show that ...

### Closings
- This completes the proof of Lemma 1. / The proof of the theorem is now complete. / ..., and Eq. (1) follows. □
- This leads to a contradiction. / Hence the statements in Section 1 are proved.
- The traditional Q.E.D. is largely replaced by the Halmos tombstone ■ or □. Write QED or Q.E.D. consistently if the journal uses it.

### Omitting proofs
- It is easy (simple, straightforward) to show that ... / Some tedious manipulation yields ... / An easy induction gives ...
- An argument similar to the one used in Section 2 shows that ...
- The remainder of the argument is analogous to that in Theorem 1 and is left to the reader.
- The proof of this theorem is not particularly difficult but will not be reproduced here. / The proof is not difficult but is too long to give here.
- This is a standard result which can be found in textbooks on probability; no proof will be given here.
- For a rigorous proof the reader is referred to [12]. / The proof is postponed until Section 8. / The rigorous proof is beyond the scope of this paper.

### Inside proofs, by function
- Reduction to a new claim, before proving it: "The theorem will be proved if we can show ..." / "If we can prove ..., then the theorem follows immediately."
- After the claim: "The theorem is now a direct consequence of what we have proved." / "The theorem follows immediately from what we have proved."
- Recalling tools: "Recall (Notice, Note, Observe) that ..."
- Special-case reduction: "Without loss of generality, we may assume ..." / "For simplicity, we may take ..."
- Similar-method reuse: "Using the same argument as in the proof of Theorem N, we can easily carry out the proof." / "We now proceed as in the proof of Theorem N."
- Contradiction: "If the statement were false, then ..." (subjunctive!) / "This contradicts the fact that ..." / "This leads to a contradiction."
- Long-proof signposts: "An outline of the proof is as follows. First, we establish that ... Our task now is to ... Our problem reduces to ... It remains to show that ... Finally, we have to show that ..."
- Obvious parts: "Since (A) and (B) are obvious, we need only prove (C)." / "It suffices to prove (C)."
- Auxiliary objects: "Let us first define (introduce, consider) ..." / "Let (Set, Put, Denote) ..."

## 5. Connectives with emphasis gradation

Combining:
- and / both ... and (neutral) — also, as well as (slight emphasis on the first item) — apart from, in addition to (stronger) — not only ... but also (emphasizes plurality) — moreover, furthermore (emphasizes what follows).

Cause / implication (weakest to strongest):
- as / because / since — consequently / therefore / thus — it follows that — due to / in view of / owing to / on account of — given ("Given the ill condition of the Jacobian, the solution is necessarily sensitive ...").
- Do not fill a page with "because" and "since"; vary. "because" takes a clause; "because of" takes a noun phrase.
- Same for "therefore/hence": vary with consequently, it follows from ... that.

Contrast / restriction:
- although (sentence head, stronger) — though (weaker, mid-sentence) — but — whereas — by contrast — except (explicit restriction) — however / on the other hand (only with two alternatives!) — nevertheless (both statements hold despite tension) — despite / in spite of — instead of vs. rather than (rather than implies a deliberate choice; instead of merely states a substitution).
- Never "Although ..., but ..." together.

## 6. Reporting figures and tables

- Figure 2 shows the structure of the exact solution. / Results of these calculations are given (shown, presented, plotted) in Figs. 1 and 2.
- The two basis functions are distinct quadratic functions, as illustrated in Figure 5.2.
- Graphically, there is no observable difference between ... / As can be seen, there is good agreement between the present result and the reference computation.
- The streamlines, shown in Fig. 3, are in good agreement with the results of Li et al. [6] and Zhang [8]; the difference in the separation points is less than 5%.
- Figure 4.1 shows a computed solution to Burgers' equation using a high-resolution method with a time step that satisfies the CFL condition. (带条件句)
- Computational results are shown in Figure 3.1, illustrating the oscillations that appear in this case, again with the first-order Godunov method. (非限制补充)
- These are seen much more clearly in Figure 3.2, which shows the same solution on a different scale. (跨图指引)

## 8. Conclusion stock phrases

## 10. Modifier families (noun / adjective / adverb)

Choose the word class that fits the syntactic slot; do not force a noun into an adverb's job. High-frequency families:

- accuracy/exactness — accurate/exact/precise — accurately/exactly; strictness — strict — strictly; stability — stable — stably; instability — unstable
- correctness/soundness — correct/sound — correctly/soundly; mistake/error — mistaken/wrong — mistakenly/wrongly
- quality — qualitative — qualitatively; quantity — quantitative — quantitatively
- basics/foundation — basic/fundamental — basically/fundamentally; completeness — complete/entire — completely/entirely
- evidence — evident — evidently; apparentness — apparent — apparently; determination — deterministic — deterministically
- succession — successive — successively; subsequence — subsequent — subsequently (successive=接连的, subsequent=随后的,勿混)
- equivalence — equivalent — equivalently; explicitness — explicit — explicitly; implicitness — implicit — implicitly
- correspondence — corresponding — correspondingly; initiation — initial — initially; caution/care — cautious/careful — cautiously/carefully
- confidence — confident — confidently; satisfaction — satisfactory — satisfactorily; coincidence — coincident — coincidentally; truth — true — truly; identification — identical — identically
- No common adverb exists — do not coin one: priority/prior, availability/available.
